How to install APP GOD

Installing takes about 10 seconds. After that, APP GOD opens from your home screen or desktop like any other app.

💻 Laptop or PC (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome.
  2. Click the Install APP GOD button, or the install icon on the right side of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. APP GOD opens in its own window.

📱 Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ then Add to Home Screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Install, then open APP GOD from your home screen.

🍏 iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open APP GOD from your home screen.

The first visit can take up to a minute while the free server wakes up. 😴➡️⚡
